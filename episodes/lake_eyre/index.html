About the diagrams

  • The opening outline starts at the closest point on the generalised shoreline to the recorded camera location.
  • River courses are generalised downstream traces. Animated river progress is illustrative along mapped channels; the flood progression and its first endpoint illustrate successive floods on a mapped channel.
  • The shading illustrates infiltration and loss. It is not a measured area or volume.
