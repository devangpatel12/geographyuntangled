Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026. This policy covers Geography Untangled Studio, the private program that the Geography Untangled YouTube channel uses to prepare, publish and review its own videos.

Who uses it

Only the channel's owner. The program runs on the owner's own computer and signs in with the owner's own Google account. No one else can sign in to it. It collects nothing from viewers of the channel or visitors to this site, and it places no cookies on anyone's device.

YouTube API Services

Geography Untangled Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it reads and does

  • The owner's own videos, captions and thumbnails (YouTube Data API): to upload the channel's videos as private with a release time, to set their titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, and to read the list of the channel's videos and its number of subscribers.
  • The owner's own channel statistics (YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs): views, watch time, how long viewers stay, and how often thumbnails are shown and clicked.
  • Public information about other channels' videos: for a topic the owner is considering, the program asks YouTube's search for videos on it and reads their titles, channel names, publication dates, lengths and view counts, and each channel's subscriber count and number of videos. These are shown to the owner exactly as YouTube provides them, beside YouTube's logo. No figure is calculated from them.

How it is used, and who sees it

The information is used only to plan, publish and review the channel's own videos, and is seen only by the owner.

Parts of the owner's own channel information (video titles and statistics) are processed by an AI assistant service the owner uses, Anthropic's Claude, on the owner's instructions, to help plan and review videos. The program's own AI jobs are never given information about other channels' videos. The owner may look at it with the help of the same AI assistant. It is not given to anyone else.

Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How long it is kept

  • The sign-in token and the owner's own channel statistics are kept on the owner's computer for as long as they are needed.
  • The titles of the owner's own videos are read afresh from YouTube each time they are needed. Earlier copies stay in the program's working files on the owner's computer.
  • Information about other channels' videos is deleted within 30 days.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time through Google's security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is removed, the owner deletes the information the program has stored from YouTube and the stored sign-in token from the owner's computer. Deleting what the program has stored does not delete anything on YouTube itself; that is done in YouTube.
